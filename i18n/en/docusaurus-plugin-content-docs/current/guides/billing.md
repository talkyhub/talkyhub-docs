# Plans and payment

TalkyHub offers two ways to pay for a plan.

## 1. Online payment (for everyone)

Pick a plan and pay by card, SBP, T-Pay or SberPay. Payment goes through
acquiring, and the plan is activated automatically once the payment is confirmed.
A fiscal receipt (54-FZ) is issued and, if you like, sent to the given email —
you can change the address before paying.

## 2. Invoice for legal entities (B2B)

If you pay on behalf of a company, fill in the billing details. Entering the INN
is enough — the rest of the details are pulled automatically from open registries.
After that an invoice with a QR code becomes available for payment via internet
banking (T-Bank B2B).

:::note Details first
The invoice option appears only after the billing details are filled in. Until
then, online payment is available.
:::

## Company lookup by INN

Looking up an organisation by INN is available as a standalone feature: the name,
address and other details are returned to auto-fill the form.

## Fiscalization

Receipts are issued through a fiscal data operator (ATOL Online). The subject of
settlement is an IT service, and the settlement method is full payment.
