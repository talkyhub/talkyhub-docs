# What's new in the app

Changes to the TalkyHub web app (app.talkyhub.ru), newest first.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-10-25

### Changed

- **The website widget preview is the real widget.** In the website channel settings the preview shows the
  same widget your visitors will see, and updates as you edit colours, texts, pre-chat fields, links and the
  greeting. Switch between desktop and phone, open and close the chat, try the pre-chat form.
- After you create a website channel, the screen with the embed snippet shows how the widget will look on your site.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-25

### Added

- **Avito (beta)** — connect an Avito account with its API keys and answer buyers from TalkyHub. The
  conversation shows the listing: photo, title, price and status ("Sold", "Removed from sale"), with "Open on
  Avito" and "Chat in Avito" links, and "Other conversations" lists everyone who asked about that listing.
- Avito conversations show the listing thumbnail and title under the buyer's name in the list.
- Replies sent from the Avito app also appear in the conversation, marked "Replied in the Avito app".

### Changed

- Sign-in and sign-up are one step: enter your email, and if there's no account yet you're offered to create
  a workspace. The separate "Create a workspace" link is gone.

### Fixed

- The language picker on the sign-in page no longer slides sideways when it opens.
- On phones the Send button fits inside the reply box.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-18

The first versioned release of the app.

### Added

- **Sign in with Yandex ID and VK ID**, below the "Continue with email" form. If the provider hasn't
  confirmed your email, confirm it once with a code; after that it's one tap.
- **Sign-in & security**, a new settings page: connect and disconnect Yandex ID and VK ID, and manage the
  trusted devices you sign in to with a passcode. Email-code sign-in always stays available.
- **Notifications** in the browser (even with the tab closed), in Telegram and in MAX. In-app and push
  switches for each event, quiet hours, and a "Send a test" button.
- **New conversation** — a notification when a customer starts a conversation in an inbox you can see (off by
  default).
- **Install the app** on your Home Screen (iPhone, Android) or desktop; the unread count shows on the app
  icon and in the tab.
- **Help** — the "?" button opens help for the current screen, what's new and documentation links without
  leaving the app.
- **Customer emails** show their formatting and the images pasted into them.
- **New email to a customer** — start an email conversation right from the conversation list.
- **Merge contacts** — fold a duplicate into the current contact.
- **Message edit history** — from the "edited" marker.

### Changed

- **Settings** are split into Account and Workspace with a permanent menu on the left; on phones, a list of
  sections. Reach them with the gear next to the bell.
- On phones, a conversation's Details open as their own tab.

### Fixed

- Dropdowns inside dialogs (e.g. "New field") no longer hide behind the dialog; arrow keys and Esc work.
- "Saved" messages now show on every settings page.
- A clear message when sign-up is closed on the server.
