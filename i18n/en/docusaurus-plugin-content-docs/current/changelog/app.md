# What's new in the app

Changes to the TalkyHub web app (app.talkyhub.ru), newest first.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-27

The first release of the TalkyHub web app.

### Features

- **Sign in with an email code**, or with **Yandex ID** and **VK ID**. One step: if you don't have an account
  yet, we offer to create a workspace. Sign-in methods and trusted devices live in "Sign-in & security".
- **Channels:** Telegram, VK, MAX, email, the website widget, **Avito (beta)** — with the listing card in the
  conversation — and **WhatsApp Business (beta)** through 360dialog or the Cloud API: the 24-hour reply window,
  approved templates, a new conversation by phone number. The **API channel** connects any messenger or app of your own.
- **Website widget:** the preview is the same widget your visitors see and updates as you edit; copy the embed
  snippet in one click. The pre-chat form can also ask your own contact fields (say, “Contract number”), saved on
  the contact; the widget's language is auto, Russian or English.
- **Home** — what needs attention: unassigned conversations, those waiting for a reply and yours; who has
  waited longest; notifications; channels that aren't receiving messages.
- **Conversations:** "Mine · Unassigned · All" tabs with counts and **smart search** — type in words ("avito
  waiting", "mine urgent", "#2340", "@oleg") and the field turns them into filters.
- **Replies:** canned responses with "/", private notes for agents, attachments, a signature. Customer emails
  show with formatting and images; start a new email to a customer right from the list. An unsent reply is kept
  as a draft, and the editor expands over the whole chat for long messages. The greeting, resolution message and
  email subject come with ready-made examples, shown as the customer will read them.
- **The conversation's controls in its header:** take it, assignee, team, priority as icons with tooltips, and
  labels. A "Chat | Client" switch opens the client view: ways to reach them, your fields, notes, SLA and past
  conversations. Messages show who wrote them and their role; yours say "You".
- **Contacts:** photos from Telegram, VK and MAX (or uploaded), links to messenger profiles, merge duplicates,
  message edit history.
- **Quoted replies** in Telegram, MAX and VK; a message that wasn't sent shows the channel's reason.
- **Notifications** in the app, in the browser (even with the tab closed), in Telegram and MAX — per event, with
  quiet hours.
- **Workspace settings:** plan, seats and channels at a glance, team, canned responses, labels (also created right
  from a conversation’s details), reports.
- **Payment** by card, СБП, T-Pay or SberPay, or by invoice for companies. The total, the discount for a longer
  term and when the plan starts are shown before you pay.
- **Install on phone and desktop:** add TalkyHub to the Home Screen or desktop; the unread count shows on the
  app icon.
- **Help** — the "?" button opens help for the current screen and "What's new" without leaving the app.
